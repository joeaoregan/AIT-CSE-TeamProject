## User Stories

### Manager

As a Manager, I want to...

- [STM28](manager.md/#stm28) Login to the system, access my account.
- [STM06](manager.md/#stm06) Add boats to inventory, create lists of Boats for users to view.
- [STM08](manager.md/#stm08) View boats in inventory, see what boats are listed.
- [STM14](manager.md/#stm14) Add prices to boats/inventory, update prices for customers.
- [STM16](manager.md/#stm16) View prices added to boats/inventory, visible to our customers.
- [STM01](manager.md/#stm01) Create employee accounts, so they can login.
- [STM07](manager.md/#stm07) Delete a boat, remove boats from the inventory.
- [STM09](manager.md/#stm09) Edit boat details, be certain the info is correct.
- [STM15](manager.md/#stm15) Delete boat prices, remove incorrect prices.
- [STM17](manager.md/#stm17) Edit pricing, make certain prices are up to date.
- [STM29](manager.md/#stm29) Log out of the system, so my account can be secure.

### Customer

As a Customer I want to...

- [CUS09](customer.md/#cus09) Search the site, see what boats are available, prices & available dates
- [CUS10](customer.md/#cus10) Register on the site, make bookings & return to where my details are saved.
- [CUS11](customer.md/#cus11) Login to the site, book a boat.
- [CUS01](customer.md/#cus01) Select a boat, book a boat for any number of days.
- [CUS07](customer.md/#cus07) Pay a deposit, confirm my booking.
- [CUS12](customer.md/#cus12) Log out of the system, no one can access my details.

### Front Desk

As a Front desk user, I want to...

- [FRO01](front-desk.md/#fro01) Login to the system, access my account.
- [FRO02](front-desk.md/#fro02) Log out of the system so account can be secure.

### Skipper

As a Skipper, I want to...

- [SKI04](skipper.md/#ski04) Log out of the system, so account can be secure.
- [SKI03](skipper.md/#ski03) Login to the system, access my account.
